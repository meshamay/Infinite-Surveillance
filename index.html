How a project goes

A clear path from “we need cameras” to “it’s working and documented.”

  1. Site visit

    We check camera spots, power, network and what you most need to protect.

  2. Plan

    You get a system design and equipment list that fits the site and budget.

  3. Install and set up

    Cameras, cabling, recorders and remote viewing, installed and configured.

  4. Test and hand over

    We verify everything works and give you the records you need.

  5. Ongoing care

    Maintenance, troubleshooting and upgrades whenever you need them.
